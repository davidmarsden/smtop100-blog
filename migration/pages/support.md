# Support Top 100

Top 100 is a community project. The website, domain and supporting apps all have running costs, and over the years managers have occasionally helped with those costs.

Any financial support is always appreciated, but contributions will always be optional and voluntary. Playing in Top 100 does not depend on paying towards the website.
