# Support Top 100

Top 100 is a community project. The website, domain and supporting apps all have running costs, and over the years managers have occasionally helped with those costs.

Any financial support is always appreciated, but contributions will always be optional and voluntary. Playing in Top 100 does not depend on paying towards the website.

## Contribute

The old WordPress support page offered four ways to help:

- **£1 one-off contribution**
- **One-time donation**
- **Monthly donation**
- **Yearly donation**

The one-time, monthly and yearly donation options allowed supporters to choose their own amount.

These options should remain available on the new site. The original buttons were provided by WordPress/Jetpack, so their payment handling cannot simply be copied into Micro.blog. The replacement page should connect these four options to the chosen payment provider while keeping the same simple voluntary-support model.
