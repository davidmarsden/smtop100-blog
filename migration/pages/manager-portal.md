# Manager Portal

**[manager.smtop100.blog](https://manager.smtop100.blog/)** is where Top 100 managers sign in. Here's what it's for, and why I'm asking everyone to claim an account.

## Why it exists

Soccer Manager used to give game world admins a way to message every manager at once. That's gone: we're now limited to 20 separate messages a day. With 100 clubs in Top 100 and another 100 in Top 100 Regen, that doesn't go far.

Meanwhile, managers have spread across WhatsApp, Facebook, Discord and Telegram. Everybody uses something different, so getting an announcement, a vacancy or a fixture reminder to everyone has become a lot of extra work.

The Manager Portal replaces all of that for admin and group chat, in one place that's private to Top 100.

## What you can do with it

**In Top 100:**

- **Group chat**, private to Top 100 managers, plus admin announcements that reach everyone at once.
- **Apply for jobs** when vacancies are advertised.
- **Register for the Youth Cup** and other tournaments.
- **Keep track of your fixtures and results**, and submit your own results.
- **Youth Cup reminders** by email, if you opt in.
- **Vote** in Community Polls and the Manager Awards.

**In Top 100 Regen** the same account covers tournament registration, fixtures and results, including the Regen Youth Cup. More Regen features will follow on the same account.

Plenty more is coming. The aim is that anything you need to do as a Top 100 manager outside the game itself happens in one place.

## What it isn't

- **It isn't a replacement for your other chats.** Use WhatsApp, Discord or anything else you like with other managers. Nothing changes there.
- **There's no private messaging between managers.** The chat is a group space, and admin messages go to managers. If you want a one-to-one conversation, carry on using whatever you use now.
- **It isn't another social network.** It's there so the admin side of Top 100 works without chasing people across five apps.

## Your account

- **One account per manager.** If you manage in both Top 100 and Top 100 Regen, one account covers both.
- **Sign-in is by email.** You get a secure link, and you can set a password afterwards if you prefer.
- **Your email address is never shown publicly** and is only used for signing in and for emails you've opted into, such as Youth Cup reminders.
- **Your claim is checked** against the current manager list for your game world, so only real managers get in.

## Claim yours

1. Go to **[manager.smtop100.blog](https://manager.smtop100.blog/)**.
2. Enter your email address and open the sign-in link we send you.
3. Choose **Top 100** or **Top 100 Regen**, pick your club and confirm your Soccer Manager name.

If you already have an account, just sign in with the same email address. Please don't create a second one.

Questions or problems? Use **[Contact / Join](/contact/)** or email **admin@smtop100.blog**.
