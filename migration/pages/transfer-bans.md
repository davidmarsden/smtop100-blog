# Transfer Bans

Transfer bans enforce Top 100's squad limit and transfer rules ([rules 4.1 and 5](https://smtop100.blog/rules/#41-squad-limit)). A manager is banned for going over the 50-player squad limit, even briefly, or for cancelling an agreed deal ([rule 5.1](https://smtop100.blog/rules/#51-agreed-deals-are-binding)). A genuine mistake on a system bid, with no prior agreement between the managers, can be cancelled within five minutes without a ban ([rule 5.2](https://smtop100.blog/rules/#52-genuine-mistakes)).

Signed-in managers can always see the up-to-date list of bans in force on the [Manager Portal](https://manager.smtop100.blog/). Don't offer deals to a club under a ban, or accept theirs.

## How bans work

During a transfer ban the only permitted deals are **cash sales to clubs outside Top 100**. A banned manager may not deal with any other Top 100 manager, even by selling for cash, until the ban ends.

A player signed or loaned in who takes a club over the limit must be sold to an external club, or the loan returned to the parent club. The club may never sign that player again, and a returned loan player can't be loaned again for 70 days (the same period as an internal transfer ban).

### Sanction levels

| Level | Transfer ban | Plus |
|---|---|---|
| 1st level offence | 1 month | sell the player or players who took the squad over 50; no re-bid (purchase or loan) |
| 2nd level offence | 3 months | the same |
| 3rd level offence | 4 months | the same |
| 4th level offence | 6 months | the same |

A manager who doesn't break the transfer rules for **six months after a ban ends moves back down one level**, and down another for each further six months.
