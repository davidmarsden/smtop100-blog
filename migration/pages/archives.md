# Posts Archive

Top 100 has been running since 2015, so there is a lot of history here.

This page is the chronological archive of the **Top 100 blog**: more than a decade of manager-written posts, club records, tournament reports, season previews and reviews, old media projects and the occasional glorious bit of nonsense.

For structured records — seasons, managers, league tables, honours, statistics and historical comparisons — use **Top 100 Stats & History** instead:

https://archive.smtop100.blog/

The two archives serve different purposes:

- **Posts Archive** — what managers wrote and published over the years.
- **Stats & History** — structured records and historical data.

The redesigned site should keep the Posts Archive easy to reach without letting eleven years of history overwhelm current activity.
