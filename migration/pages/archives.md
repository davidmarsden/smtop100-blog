# Archive

Top 100 has been running since 2015, so there is a lot of history here.

For structured records — seasons, managers, league tables, honours, statistics and historical comparisons — use the Top 100 Archive:

https://archive.smtop100.blog/

The blog itself also preserves more than a decade of manager-written posts: club records, tournament reports, season previews and reviews, old media projects and the occasional glorious bit of nonsense.

The redesigned site will keep that material available without making the historical archive dominate the current site navigation.
