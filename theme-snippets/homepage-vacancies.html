How to apply for a Top 100 club

With 100 clubs, a place only opens when a manager leaves. Vacancies are published with a closing date, and every application is scored on the public record.

Already a Top 100 manager

  1. Sign in to the Manager Portal.
  2. Open Apply for a club and tick the vacancies you want, in order of preference, before the closing date. One club is fine.
  3. Gold membership is required to apply.
Apply in the Manager Portal →

New or returning manager

  1. Sign in to the Manager Portal with your email.
  2. Choose Join the waiting list for Top 100, Top 100 Regen or both, and accept the rules agreement.
  3. Clubs still open after current managers have applied are offered from the list: returning Top 100 managers first, then by waiting-list score. New managers are encouraged to start in Top 100 Regen.
