# Write for Top 100: the new Publishing Desk

For years, contributing to Top 100 usually meant getting access to whatever publishing system the site happened to be using at the time.

That made sense when the site and the publishing system were basically the same thing.

They are not anymore.

The new route for manager and contributor writing is the **Publishing Desk**:

[https://write.smtop100.blog/](https://write.smtop100.blog/)

## Why a separate Publishing Desk?

Because writing a post and administering the whole website are different jobs.

A contributor should not need broad access to the underlying publishing platform just to send in a match report, season piece or opinion article.

The Publishing Desk gives us a cleaner middle layer: contributors can submit work through a purpose-built route, while the main site remains the public publishing home.

## The main blog is still where articles live

The Publishing Desk is not a second Top 100 blog.

Published pieces still belong on:

[https://smtop100.blog/](https://smtop100.blog/)

The Desk is simply the route used to get writing into that site.

That distinction matters because it keeps the public archive coherent. Readers do not need to care which tool was used behind the scenes.

## Author credit matters

When we moved the old WordPress archive, one of the things we had to recover was original author information.

Top 100 has never been written by one person. Managers and contributors have produced a huge amount of the site over the years, and that work should stay attached to the people who wrote it.

The new workflow is designed around that principle rather than treating everything as though it came from one generic site account.

## Manager identity helps here too

The Publishing Desk can use the same approved manager identity as the rest of the ecosystem.

That means we do not need to invent another login system just for writing.

It also gives us a cleaner way to know who is submitting something without asking contributors to repeatedly re-enter the same identity details.

## What should people write?

Pretty much the things that have always made Top 100 worth reading.

Match reports. Season reviews. Predictions. Club news. Tactical nonsense. Manager interviews. Arguments. Long-form pieces. Short updates. Things that are funny because somebody took a fictional football management game far too seriously.

The technology has changed. The point has not.

## Why not just let everyone post directly?

Because a publishing workflow still benefits from a little structure.

The Desk gives us room for review, formatting, author credit and publication without handing over the keys to the whole site.

That should make contributing easier, not more bureaucratic.

## Where to go

If you want to write something for Top 100:

**[Open the Publishing Desk](https://write.smtop100.blog/)**

The old WordPress dashboard is no longer the front door for contributors.

The new one has a much better sign on it: **Write**.
