# Explore Top 100: where everything lives now

Moving Top 100 was the easy bit.

The harder bit was making sure people could still find anything afterwards.

That is why we built **Explore**:

[https://smtop100.blog/explore/](https://smtop100.blog/explore/)

Explore is the map of the new Top 100 ecosystem. It is not another archive and it is not a replacement homepage. It is the place to go when you know roughly what you want, but not which part of Top 100 now owns it.

## Why Explore exists

Top 100 used to ask one website to do nearly everything.

That was manageable when most of the site was just posts and pages. It is less sensible now that we have dedicated systems for tournaments, statistics, voting, Manager Awards, manager accounts and publishing.

Those things are better as separate tools. But separate tools create a new problem: URLs.

Nobody should have to remember six subdomains just to find a Youth Cup table or the Hall of Fame.

Explore fixes that.

## Youth Cup and World Club Cup

The two biggest competitions get their own obvious routes from Explore.

Both live within the Tournament Manager at:

[https://tournaments.smtop100.blog/](https://tournaments.smtop100.blog/)

From there you can get to fixtures, groups, knockout rounds, results and whatever competition is currently active.

The important change is that we no longer hide everything behind a generic **Tournaments** label and expect people to know what is inside it. Youth Cup and World Club Cup are important enough to be named.

## Stats & History

Top 100 has years of accumulated history, and it deserves something better than a pile of old blog posts.

Explore links directly to:

[https://archive.smtop100.blog/](https://archive.smtop100.blog/)

That is the structured history side of Top 100: seasons, records, clubs, managers and long-term data.

The ordinary Posts Archive on the main blog still exists too, but it answers a different question. The Posts Archive tells you what we published. Stats & History tells you what happened.

## Community Polls

Community Polls live at:

[https://vote.smtop100.blog/](https://vote.smtop100.blog/)

Public results can be viewed there without an account. When a manager vote is open, eligible managers sign in to cast their own ballot.

That is separate from Manager Awards, even though both now use the same verified identity underneath.

## Manager Awards

The Manager Awards site lives here:

[https://awards.smtop100.blog/](https://awards.smtop100.blog/)

It combines current Awards activity with the historical side: previous winners, records and the Hall of Fame.

Explore keeps Awards visible as its own part of Top 100 rather than burying it under Polls.

## Search and Categories

We have more than a decade of writing. Sometimes the thing you want is not a competition or an app at all — it is an old article.

Explore therefore links directly to site Search and to the curated category structure.

The categories are deliberately much tighter than the old WordPress taxonomy. Historical metadata has not been erased, but we are no longer making the main navigation carry every category we have ever created.

## Write

If you want to contribute something to Top 100, Explore takes you to the Publishing Desk:

[https://write.smtop100.blog/](https://write.smtop100.blog/)

That is the new route for manager and contributor writing. No WordPress dashboard required.

## Subscribe

Subscribe is for following published Top 100 writing by email.

That is different from personalised Manager Portal reminders. One follows the site; the other follows things that belong specifically to your manager account, such as your Youth Cup fixtures.

Keeping those two ideas separate makes both easier to understand.

## Support

Support means exactly that: optional financial help towards the costs of running Top 100 and the apps around it.

It is not the technical-help desk.

If you need help with an account, a submission or something not working, use Contact / Join instead.

## The idea is simple

You should not need to understand the architecture to use Top 100.

You should be able to think:

- “I want my Youth Cup fixture.”
- “I want to see who won that old season.”
- “I need to vote.”
- “I want to write something.”
- “Where are the Manager Awards?”

…and get there in a couple of clicks.

That is what Explore is for.

If the new Top 100 feels bigger than the old one, that is because it is. Explore is there to make sure bigger does not mean harder to use.
