# The Transfer League: who trades with whom in Top 100

Top 100 has always been a trading game world. Now we can measure it. Every completed transfer of Season 28, from 23 June to the last day of the season, is counted, and the AI has crunched the numbers: who traded most, who traded with whom, and which deals stood out.

## The numbers

Season 28 saw **3,227 completed transfers**. Most were with clubs outside Top 100, but **391 were between two Top 100 managers**, and those are the ones that matter here.

- **93 different managers** sold a player to another Top 100 manager, and **81** bought one.
- Between them they paid **4.20bn** in fees.
- The typical (median) fee was **5.0m**.
- **237** of the 391 deals included other players as well as the main one, and **68** were straight swaps with no cash at all.
- **81** of them, one in five, came after the last league games on 5 November, worth 907m between them.

It matters for more than bragging rights. Trading with other Top 100 managers is part of the activity score used in reappointment reviews and vacancy applications ([rule 3](https://smtop100.blog/rules/#3-manager-activity-and-conduct)).

The two biggest fees came in the last days of the season. Wayne Bullough paid 150m in cash for T. Ernst, a 23-year-old rated 83, on the season's final day. Two days earlier, Luciano Catalano paid the same, plus L. Hornicek, for goalkeeper M. Svilar.

The Godfather was the market's big mover, buying Caci for 100m and selling Mayoral and Simeone for 165.5m between them.

## The final ten days

Once the league games were over, the market came alive. The best players in the game world changed hands for small fees, with top players going the other way:

- **E. Haaland (96, 26)**, Hellas Verona to Borussia Dortmund, from Sir Stephen Beddows (God) to Scott Mckenzie, for 20m plus **J. Bellingham** (now 95, 23). Two of the highest-rated players in the game world swapped clubs in one deal.
- **F. Vitinha (95, 26)**, Sporting CP to Chelsea, from Carl Martin to James Mckenzie, for 12m plus **M. Caicedo** (now 93, 25).
- **K. De Bruyne (93, 35)**, Hamburger SV to Bayern München, from David Marsden to Bojan H, a straight swap for **M. Jauregizar** (now 89, 23).
- **C. Ronaldo (91, 41)**, Atlético Madrid to Real Madrid, from simon thomas to Jamie Alldridge, for 0.29m plus youngster **D. Santos Daiber** (now 73, 19).

Sir Stephen Beddows (God) rebuilt Hellas Verona at speed: 14 deals with other Top 100 managers after taking over, selling Haaland, R. Neves (91) and G. Di Lorenzo (91) and bringing in G. Simeone (91), M. Retegui (91) and M. van de Ven (91). Zé Quim's Swansea City took Neves, Di Lorenzo and G. Martinelli (91).

## The stars who moved earlier

The big fees and the big names rarely went together. Earlier in the season, too, the best players changed hands for much less, usually with players going the other way:

- **F. Wirtz (94, 23)**, Leicester City to Genoa CFC, from Ash L to Frank Hirst. Genoa paid 30m plus **M. Rogers** (now 92) and **A. Santos** (now 89).
- **L. Díaz (93, 29)**, Barcelona to 1. FC Köln, from Adam to Gursimran Brar, for 28m. It was part of a wider exchange: across several transfers this season, **M. Cancellieri** (86), **Á. Baena** (91) and **J. Derry** (73, 19) went from Köln to Barcelona.
- **P. Foden (93, 26)**, FC Porto to 1. FC Köln, from André Guerra to Gursimran Brar, for 28m. In the same exchange **J. García** (92) went from Köln to Porto, and across the season **E. Moraes** (91) joined Foden at Köln while **H. Haraldsson** (88) moved the other way.
- **C. Palmer (93, 24)**, Aston Villa to São Paulo FC, from David Inglis to Scott Mckenzie. São Paulo paid 2m plus **N. Williams** (now 92) and **M. Akliouche** (now 90).
- **A. Griezmann (93, 35)**, Cruzeiro to Bayern München, from Steven Allington to Bojan H. Bayern paid 0.25m plus **R. Lewandowski** (now 93, 38) and **M. Alleyne** (now 80).
- **J. Timber (92, 25)**, Leicester City to Barcelona, from Ash L to Adam, in one of the season's biggest swaps. Over two turns, **J. Timber**, **M. Cunha** (91), **A. Tchouaméni** (93) and **J. Hinshelwood** (88) went to Barcelona, and **E. Anderson** (91), **Á. Baena** (91), **A. Gray** (87) and **N. Schlotterbeck** (92) went to Leicester.
- **E. Martínez (93, 34)**, the joint second-highest-rated goalkeeper in the world, Standard Liège to Hamburger SV, from Raymond Reddington to David Marsden. A straight swap for **L. Sané** (now 91) and **Y. Bounou** (now 90).

Gursimran Brar brought three 92–93 rated players to Köln (Díaz, Foden and Havertz). André Guerra got García for Foden and added X. Simons (91) from Genoa on the way to Porto's Division 1 title.

## How it's counted

- A deal counts as being between two Top 100 managers when both clubs are Top 100 clubs with a manager at the time.
- Ratings and ages are as they were at the time of the deal.
- It covers Season 28 from 22 June, the day before the season began, to 16 November, its last day.
