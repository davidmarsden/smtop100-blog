# The season so far, in numbers

Every season, **Mister TRX** does Top 100 a huge favour. Before a ball is kicked he rates every squad, predicts where each club will finish, and at the end of the season reviews who beat the prediction and who fell short.

Those predictions and reviews are some of the most-read posts we publish. Thanks to Malcolm's dataset, every season from S2 to S27 is already on the Stats Archive: squad strength, predicted finish, actual finish and value added.

One thing has been missing, though. **What does it look like right now, halfway through a season?**

Now you can check.

## Live division stats

The Stats Archive page now opens with this season's numbers for every division, updated whenever the league syncs from Soccer Manager:

[https://archive.smtop100.blog/#stats-archive](https://archive.smtop100.blog/#stats-archive)

For every club you can see:

- **Squad strength (ETOT)**, using Mister TRX's own formula: best goalkeeper, best four defenders, best four midfielders, best two forwards and the top-18 average, weighted exactly as in his predictions.
- **Squad value**, and **points per £10m**: who is getting the most out of the least.
- **Average age**, both for the whole squad and weighted by appearances, which is the age of the players a manager actually picks.

Then pick what to rank the division by: the league table, the strongest squads, the youngest and oldest teams, the most valuable squads, or the Moneyball table.

## Value added, as the season happens

From Season 29, each club's strength is recorded automatically on opening day. That gives each division its pre-season order, built from the same data and the same formula Mister TRX uses.

That unlocks two more rankings:

- **Value added so far**: predicted position minus current position. Plus numbers mean a club is beating its squad; minus numbers mean it isn't (yet).
- **Most improved squad**: how much each club's strength has changed since the season started, through transfers, rating changes or both.

Nothing changes about Mister TRX's posts. His predictions, his reviews and his take on the season stay his. The stats page just means the numbers behind them are there whenever you want to look: before his preview, after his review, or in the middle of a relegation scrap at 2am.

## Awards so far

Under each division you'll also find the season's individual standouts so far:

- top scorer
- most assists
- best average rating (10+ appearances)
- best young player (21 or under, 10+ appearances)
- oldest regular (10+ appearances)
