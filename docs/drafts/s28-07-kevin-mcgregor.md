# Kevin McGregor: from Division 5 to Division 1 in five seasons

Kevin McGregor leaves Top 100 after 21 seasons. This season at Juventus is one to forget, but it shouldn't overshadow one of the great climbs in Top 100's history, or the trophies that came with it.

## The early years (Seasons 6–10)

Kevin started at Beşiktaş in Season 6, then had two seasons at Newcastle United in Division 2. Newcastle won the Top 100 Shield with him in Season 9.

## Division 5 to Division 1 (Seasons 13–19)

Kevin returned in Season 13 at Feyenoord, in Division 5. What followed was five seasons of promotions, at two clubs:

| Season | Club | Division | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | Feyenoord | 5 | **Champions** |
| 15 | Feyenoord | 4 | **Play-off winners** |
| 16 | West Bromwich Albion | 3 | 9th |
| 17 | West Bromwich Albion | 3 | **Champions** |
| 18 | West Bromwich Albion | 2 | **Champions** |
| 19 | West Bromwich Albion | 1 | 11th |

Three titles and four promotions, ending in Division 1, where West Brom finished a comfortable 11th.

## Juventus (Seasons 20–28)

In Season 20 Kevin took over Juventus. In his second season there they finished 3rd in Division 1, his best finish. Juventus were in Division 1 for all nine of his seasons. He won the Top 100 Shield in Season 24 and the Top 100 Cup in Season 26.

## Honours

- Top 100 Shield, Season 9 (Newcastle United)
- Division 5 champions, Season 14 (Feyenoord)
- Division 4 play-off winners, Season 15 (Feyenoord)
- Division 3 champions, Season 17 (West Bromwich Albion)
- Division 2 champions, Season 18 (West Bromwich Albion)
- Top 100 Shield, Season 24 (Juventus)
- Top 100 Cup, Season 26 (Juventus)

Thank you, Kevin, and good luck.
