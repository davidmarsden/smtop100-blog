# Season 28: champions, promotions and farewells

Season 28 went to the last night in almost every division. Here's how it finished, in the managers' own words from the newsfeed.

## Champions

![Season 28 champions: FC Porto, SL Benfica, Sampdoria, SSC Napoli and PSV](https://tournaments.smtop100.blog/roundup/s28/s28-champions.webp)

**Division 1: FC Porto (André Guerra).** 87 points, top all season, with Chelsea four points behind. It's Porto's second Division 1 title.

> "FC Porto is Division 1 Champion!!! What a night, what a campaign! We have been at the top the entire season, but Chelsea pushed us to the limit almost till the end, kudos to James, a top rival. Winning Division 1 one time is a big thing, but to win it twice is an even bigger thing, proving we were not a one season wonder."

A few days later Porto added the World Club Cup, beating SC Internacional 1-0 in both legs.

> "We are the Champions… of the World! … What a week, what a season!"

**Division 2: SL Benfica (Carlos Miranda).** Champions with 70 points, four clear of Udinese.

**Division 3: Sampdoria (Sir Stephen Beddows).** Champions by a point, with a little help from Málaga, who beat second-placed Ajax 4-3 on the final night.

> "Champions! Just!"

JWD at Málaga: "Our win handed Sampdoria the Division 3 title… Congratulations to Sampdoria, I'll be expecting a crate of something nice in the post."

**Division 4: SSC Napoli (Alessio Tonato).** Napoli went into the last game behind and came out on top, a point ahead of Valencia and three ahead of Lokomotiv Moskva.

> "Unbelievable result!!! Naples manages to win the last game of the season, and with a serie of unthinkable results from opponents, gets top of the league in division 4 and wins!!! … Second division win in 3 seasons."

Tonian McGoogan at Valencia: "Absolutely gutted to lose out on the Div 4 title tonight… well played to Napoli who took it on the night."

**Division 5: PSV (Chris East).** Champions with 86 points and a goal difference of +42.

## Promoted

![Going up: the top three and play-off winners from Divisions 2 to 5](https://tournaments.smtop100.blog/roundup/s28/s28-promoted.webp)

Four clubs go up from each of Divisions 2 to 5: the top three, and the play-off winners.

| From | Promoted | Play-offs |
|---|---|---|
| Division 2 | SL Benfica, Udinese Calcio, West Bromwich Albion | Genoa CFC (Frank Hirst) |
| Division 3 | Sampdoria, Ajax, Zenit Saint Petersburg | AS Monaco (Jay Jones) |
| Division 4 | SSC Napoli, Valencia CF, Lokomotiv Moskva | Beşiktaş JK (yamil Mc02) |
| Division 5 | PSV, AZ Alkmaar, Atalanta BC | US Sassuolo (Luciano Catalano) |

Neil Frankland, Udinese: "Nice to win another promotion. Hello division 1 it's been a while."

Greg Bilboa, West Bromwich Albion: "Back to back promotions for West Brom as we scrape 3rd place with a 1 - 1 draw against Flamengo. After a rough start to life in Division 2 the form picked up, and despite knowing how tough Division 1 is, delighted to give it another crack."

Alessandro Ioli, Ajax: "Second consecutive promotion for the Lancers!!! GoAjaxGo!!!"

Mohammed Ajmal, AZ Alkmaar: "Promoted to Div4, disappointed though to have not won the title."

### Play-offs

**Division 2: Genoa CFC (Frank Hirst).** Genoa finished 7th, the last play-off place, and beat Bayer Leverkusen in the final.

Marco G, Bayer Leverkusen: "Bayer end their season today with a loss in the playoff final. We dominated the match but we had to face a very hard luck (2 shots conceded and 2 goals), that's the game."

**Division 3: AS Monaco (Jay Jones).** Monaco also came through from 7th. They beat Hoffenheim on penalties in the semi-final and Dnipro on penalties in the final.

> "Monaco have a 3rd successive promotion!! As with the last league match between Dnipro this play-off final could of gone either way as we beat them on pens after a 2-2 90min draw with goals from Rensch & Castellanos. When i took over Monaco they'd just been relegated to the 5th Division."

**Division 4: Beşiktaş JK (yamil Mc02).** Beşiktaş won 2-0 at Independiente on the way through.

> "The goal was achieved! Besiktas is promoted to Division 3."

Gianluca Ghio, Independiente: "Well done Yamil. Independiente had clearly the higher rated team but this means nothing when you are on the pitch."

**Division 5: US Sassuolo (Luciano Catalano).** Another 7th-placed side: Sassuolo beat Lazio in the semi-final and then edged Shakhtar Donetsk in the final.

> "Grandissima impresa Sassuolo… si aggiudica prima la semifinale contro la Lazio e poi batte di misura lo Shaktar e sale in quarta divisione!!!!!!" (A huge achievement: Sassuolo won the semi-final against Lazio, then edged past Shakhtar and go up to Division 4.)

Mark Deadman, Shakhtar Donetsk: "A very good season for us comes to an end, just missing out of promotion."

## Survival

Chris Meida, CSKA Moskva: "Big big big big win as we stay up in d1 again!"

Glen Mullan, RCD Espanyol: "We stayed up against the odds, won our 3rd youth cup in a row, in what's been a great season… It's a very young team, but we wouldn't swap it for any other."

## Farewells

Four long-serving managers are leaving Top 100:

![Dario Saviano: 24 seasons, 16 in Division 1 with Borussia Dortmund](https://tournaments.smtop100.blog/farewells/s28/farewell-dario.webp)

![Kevin McGregor: from Division 5 to Division 1 in five seasons](https://tournaments.smtop100.blog/farewells/s28/farewell-kevin.webp)

![Gav Harmer: 17 seasons at Atlético Madrid](https://tournaments.smtop100.blog/farewells/s28/farewell-gav.webp)

![Luís André Libras-Boas: 27 seasons, five Division 1 titles with Hellas Verona](https://tournaments.smtop100.blog/farewells/s28/farewell-luis.webp)

- **Dario Saviano**, after 24 seasons, the last 16 of them in Division 1 with Borussia Dortmund. His final act was winning the Top 100 Shield. [Dario Saviano: 24 seasons, 16 in Division 1](https://smtop100.blog/2026/11/16/dario-saviano-seasons-in-division/)
- **Kevin McGregor**, after 23 seasons, three league titles and nine seasons at Juventus. [Kevin McGregor: from Division 5 to Division 1 in five seasons](https://smtop100.blog/2026/11/16/kevin-mcgregor-from-division-to/)
- **Gav Harmer**, after 28 seasons, the last 17 at Atlético Madrid. [Why Gav hasn't been reappointed at Atlético Madrid](https://smtop100.blog/2026/11/06/why-gav-hasnt-been-reappointed/)
- **Luís André Libras-Boas**, after 27 seasons, almost all of them at Hellas Verona: five Division 1 titles, four in a row, and 26 honours. His last was this season's Top 100 Cup. [Luís André Libras-Boas: Hellas Verona's champion](https://smtop100.blog/2026/11/16/lus-andr-librasboas-hellas-veronas/)
