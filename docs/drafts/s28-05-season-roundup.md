# Season 28: champions, promotions and farewells

Season 28 went to the last night in almost every division. Here's how it finished, in the managers' own words from the newsfeed.

## Champions

**Division 1: FC Porto (André Guerra).** 87 points, top all season, with Chelsea four points behind. It's Porto's second Division 1 title.

> "FC Porto is Division 1 Champion!!! What a night, what a campaign! We have been at the top the entire season, but Chelsea pushed us to the limit almost till the end, kudos to James, a top rival. Winning Division 1 one time is a big thing, but to win it twice is an even bigger thing, proving we were not a one season wonder."

A few days later Porto added the World Club Cup, beating SC Internacional 1-0 in both legs.

> "We are the Champions… of the World! … What a week, what a season!"

**Division 2: SL Benfica (Carlos Miranda).** Champions with 70 points, four clear of Udinese.

**Division 3: Sampdoria (Sir Stephen Beddows).** Champions by a point, with a little help from Málaga, who beat second-placed Ajax 4-3 on the final night.

> "Champions! Just!"

The FM at Málaga: "Our win handed Sampdoria the Division 3 title… Congratulations to Sampdoria, I'll be expecting a crate of something nice in the post."

**Division 4: SSC Napoli (Alessio Tonato).** Napoli went into the last game behind and came out on top, a point ahead of Valencia and three ahead of Lokomotiv Moskva.

> "Unbelievable result!!! Naples manages to win the last game of the season, and with a serie of unthinkable results from opponents, gets top of the league in division 4 and wins!!! … Second division win in 3 seasons."

Tonian McGoogan at Valencia: "Absolutely gutted to lose out on the Div 4 title tonight… well played to Napoli who took it on the night."

**Division 5: PSV (Chris East).** Champions with 86 points and a goal difference of +42.

## Survival

Chris Meida, CSKA Moskva: "Big big big big win as we stay up in d1 again!"

Glen Mullan, RCD Espanyol: "We stayed up against the odds, won our 3rd youth cup in a row, in what's been a great season… It's a very young team, but we wouldn't swap it for any other."
