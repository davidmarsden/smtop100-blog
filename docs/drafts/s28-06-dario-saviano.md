# Dario Saviano: 24 seasons, 16 in Division 1

> "After almost 17 years it's time to leave the game. This GW has been the only reason not to leave before, thank you all."

Dario Saviano is leaving Top 100 after 24 seasons. He finishes the way few managers get to: with a trophy, the Top 100 Shield, in his last season.

## Levante: from Division 5 to Division 2 (Seasons 5–12)

Dario arrived in Season 5 at Levante UD in Division 5, and went up through the play-offs in his first season.

After three seasons in Division 4, Levante reached Division 3. In Season 11 they won it, as champions, and in Season 12 they finished 7th in Division 2.

## Borussia Dortmund: 16 seasons at the top (Seasons 13–28)

In Season 13 Dario moved to Borussia Dortmund in Division 1. He never left it. Sixteen seasons in Division 1 without a relegation puts him among a handful of managers in Top 100's history.

Early on, Dortmund beat the predictions. Later, they were among the favourites to win the title:

| Season | Finish | Predicted |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | 6th | 9th |

Season 23's runners-up spot was Dortmund's best finish under him, and Seasons 21 to 24 were four in a row in the top five.

## Honours

- Division 5 play-off winners, Season 5 (Levante)
- Division 3 champions, Season 11 (Levante)
- SMFA Shield, Season 18 (Dortmund)
- Charity Shield, Season 24 (Dortmund)
- Top 100 Shield, Season 28 (Dortmund)
