# Subscribe, Support and staying in the loop

There are now several ways to stay connected to Top 100, and they do different jobs.

That is useful, but only if the difference is obvious.

So here is the short version:

**Subscribe** follows the site.

**My Matches reminders** follow things that belong specifically to you.

**Support** helps pay for the running costs.

They are not three versions of the same thing.

## Subscribe: follow what Top 100 publishes

The Subscribe page is here:

[https://smtop100.blog/subscribe/](https://smtop100.blog/subscribe/)

That is the normal route if you want Top 100 posts delivered by email rather than remembering to check the site.

It follows the editorial side of Top 100: news, features, manager writing and whatever else we publish on the main blog.

You do not need a manager account to subscribe.

## My Matches reminders: follow your own responsibilities

Personal reminders are different.

A manager account can know which club belongs to you and which fixture is yours. That makes it possible to send useful account-specific reminders rather than another general newsletter.

The first version of that is live for Top 100 Youth Cup fixtures.

Managers can opt in to new-fixture, day-before and fixture-day reminders from **My Matches**:

[https://manager.smtop100.blog/](https://manager.smtop100.blog/)

Those reminders are not enabled just because you subscribe to the blog, and they are not enabled just because you have a manager account.

They are explicit opt-in settings of their own.

That distinction matters, especially when the reminder is connected to something you are responsible for doing.

My Matches also translates the fixture into simple action language: **YOU SEND** if you need to send the friendly request, **THEY SEND** if your opponent should send it and you are waiting for them.

## A reminder is not the rule

Youth Cup reminders are there to help managers, not to replace the competition rules.

For Saturday Youth Cup fixtures, the Soccer Manager match request must still be sent **before Wednesday**.

Do not rely on a later reminder as your trigger to arrange the game.

The system can nudge you. It cannot take responsibility for the fixture on your behalf.

## Support: help with the costs

Support is here:

[https://smtop100.blog/support/](https://smtop100.blog/support/)

Support means optional financial contributions towards the costs of keeping Top 100 and its supporting apps running.

It is not a paid membership tier and it does not unlock hidden content.

Top 100's public resources stay public, and manager tools are based on manager identity rather than whether somebody has contributed financially.

## Support is not technical support

The word can be confusing, so this is worth spelling out too.

If you need help claiming an account, submitting something, joining Top 100 or fixing a problem, Support is not the page you need.

Use Contact / Join instead.

Financial support helps keep the lights on. Contact / Join is where actual help requests belong.

## Why separate all three?

Because consent should be specific.

Somebody who wants new blog posts by email has not necessarily asked for fixture reminders.

Somebody who wants fixture reminders has not necessarily asked for every blog post.

And neither of those things should imply somebody wants to contribute financially.

Keeping them separate makes the system less clever in the best possible way: each switch does what it says.

So, depending on what you want:

- **Read Top 100 by email:** [Subscribe](https://smtop100.blog/subscribe/)
- **Manage your own fixtures and reminders:** [My Matches](https://manager.smtop100.blog/)
- **Help with running costs:** [Support](https://smtop100.blog/support/)

Three doors. Three different jobs.
